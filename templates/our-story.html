Who We Are

The Capati Family: A Legacy of Love and Service in Marikina

Our family has been rooted in Marikina since the 1970s. We are six siblings raised in a home built on the values of loyalty, hard work, and devotion — values modeled by our late parents, Pedro and Marina.

📷 Photo — Parents' 50th Anniversary with Grandchildren

The Heart of Golden Family Homecare

A Lifetime of Commitment: Our parents were married for 54 years, teaching us that true care is a lifelong commitment.

Engineering and Heart: With our father Pedro, an electrical engineer, and our mother Marina, a dedicated housewife, our home was a blend of professional discipline and warm, nurturing comfort — with home-cooked meals lovingly prepared by our Mama Mina.

Honoring "Mama Mina": We chose to launch this facility on June 9, 2026, to honor the first death anniversary of our mother's passing — turning our personal journey of love and loss into a mission of service for other families.

📷 Photo — Mommy and Daddy

Why We Started GFH

We understand the "Sandwich Generation" because we are part of this community. Our mission — to deliver professional and compassionate care that feels like home — is a direct tribute to the environment our parents created for us for over five decades.

When we say we will care for your family like our own, we mean it. We bring 50 years of Marikina heritage and the collective heart of six siblings to ensure your loved ones live with the dignity, trust, and professionalism they deserve. Just like how we cared for our Papa and Mama.
