About Us

Who We Are

Golden Circle Homecare is a senior homecare facility in Marikina, dedicated to providing professional medical support in a warm, family-centered environment. We were founded with a simple purpose: to bridge the gap between clinical care and genuine human comfort.

We serve families who want the best for their loved ones—offering peace of mind through 24/7 attentive care, while preserving the dignity, independence, and happiness of every resident. Here, your family becomes part of ours.
